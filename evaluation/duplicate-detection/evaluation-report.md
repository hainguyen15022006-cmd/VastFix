## Ranking on eligible candidate lists

The denominator is **16 positive provisional cases that retain at least one eligible duplicate** after excluding closed and merged source Issues and applying the current backend region rule. Recall is the fraction of relevant IDs in the first 3 or 5; MRR uses the first relevant rank. Metrics are exploratory because the labels have not been reviewed.

| Method | Recall@3 | Recall@5 | MRR |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Location only | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.688 |
| Keyword only | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.781 |
| `baseline-v1` | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.844 |

The baseline MRR by pair type was 0.750 on 8 English-query/Vietnamese-candidate cases, 1.000 on 2 Vietnamese-query/English-candidate cases, 0.750 on 2 Vietnamese/Vietnamese cases, and 1.000 on 1 English/English case. Three cases have relevant candidates in both languages and are reported as mixed (MRR 1.000). These groups are too small for stable subgroup conclusions.

The high Recall@3 reflects short, curated candidate lists; it is not evidence of high recall in a full campus database. The baseline put a negative first in five positive cases: C07 (AC-2 noise vs leak), C10 (door D2 vs D1), C12 (yard litter vs leaking irrigation), C13 (wet corridor vs litter), and C14 (another H2-03 fault vs a short outage description). Their relevant reports still appeared within the first three. These are examples of **incorrect top-ranked suggestions**, not automatic duplicate decisions.

## FastAPI suggestion behavior

`/duplicates` was exercised with actual score threshold and `limit=5`, after the local eligibility filter. It returned at least one relevant suggestion for **16/16 positive cases**. It also returned suggestions in **2/5 negative-only cases**. That behavior is possible under the contract because a suggestion is a ranked candidate, not a confirmed duplicate. All 21 responses passed the checked invariants: valid request ID, at most five suggestions, unique candidate IDs, score range, descending scores, and no unknown IDs. The empty case returned no suggestions.

This is an in-process FastAPI test, not an end-to-end `POST /api/duplicate-suggestions` test. The backend query, authentication, database state, fallback on AI failure, and frontend mapping still require integration testing by Chúc and Hoàng Anh. The current outdoor eligibility approximation follows the inspected backend branch's unpinned outdoor fallback. It is not proof that a live database returns the same candidate list.

## AI label review and recalculation 23 November 2026

The generated source and its historical results remain unchanged. `dataset.ai-reviewed.json` records an explicit AI review of all 61 pairs: 20 duplicate, 31 non-duplicate and ten uncertain relationships. Fourteen cases have complete AI-reviewed labels; eight cases are deferred because identifying details, symptom relationships or occurrence boundaries are not established by the descriptions. The rationale and missing information for every pair appear in `label-review-2026-11-23.json` and the Vietnamese AI label review report. No case is marked HUMAN_REVIEWED.

Evaluation excludes each uncertain case as a whole. On the nine eligible positive cases remaining, baseline and E5 both produce Recall@3=1.000, Recall@5=1.000, MRR=0.889. Location-only MRR is 0.833; keyword-only MRR is 0.889. `results-ai-reviewed-2026-11-23.json` contains full outputs. The baseline API passes all checked invariants on 14 decided cases, finds relevant suggestions in 9/9 positive cases and returns suggestions in 2/5 negative-only/empty cases. Three of those five are empty initially or after closed-issue filtering. This is not a field false-positive-rate estimate.

These scores use a different subset from the 16 positive provisional cases in the 22 November output; do not call the higher baseline number a model improvement. The review did not use hidden incident keys to force ambiguous texts into positive or negative labels. All 41 original report objects remain unchanged, and the generated fixture can still be rebuilt exactly.
